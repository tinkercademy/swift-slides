# Navigating

## And editing pages

---

<!-- .slide: class="layout-steps-media" -->
## Navigating the journal

- Slide manager on the left shows a preview of all pages
  - Tap on one to jump to that page in the journal
- Tap on the ▸ to the left of the previews to reveal grouped pages, or ▾ to close groups
- Tap on **Edit** in the top right to make changes

![Navigating the journal](/markdown/track_a/assets/navigating-journal.png)

---

<!-- .slide: class="layout-steps-media" -->
## Editing your journal

- To edit your journal, tap on the EDIT button at the top-right hand corner of the screen
- Double tap on the text boxes to edit them
- *Or* use your Apple Pencil to draw on them
- This is your journal, so make it yours!

![Editing your journal](/markdown/track_a/assets/editing-journal.jpg)

---

<!-- .slide: class="layout-steps-media" -->
## Duplicate Slides

- Within the App Journal, you may need to duplicate slides
- This is handy if you want to type down additional notes, annotate, or just have somewhere to draw things.
- To do so, tap on the slide in the navigator and select "Duplicate".

![Duplicate Slides](/markdown/track_a/assets/duplicate-slides.png)

# Collaborating

---

<!-- .slide: class="layout-steps-media" -->
## Keynote Collaboration

- To get started, tap on the (export) icon in the top right corner
- Make sure you set the document to **Collaborate**, not **Send Copy** in the drop-down box.
- Next, share the link by using:
  - **AirDrop**: If in the same room
  - **Messages**: If on iMessage
  - **Mail**: Using email address
  - **Invite with link**: Paste elsewhere

![Keynote Collaboration](/markdown/track_a/assets/keynote-collab.png)

---

<!-- .slide: class="layout-steps-media" -->
## Tip: Let Anyone Edit

- Consider using this tip, to avoid having to set up permissions.
- When sharing, tap on the arrow next to **Only invited people can edit**, and consider changing to **Anyone with the link**
- Just be careful not to share the link somewhere else!

![Let Anyone Edit](/markdown/track_a/assets/let-anyo-edit.png)

---

<!-- .slide: class="layout-steps-media" -->
## Sharing Options

- Once a document is shared, you'll see a new button show up (person-icon)
- Tap on it to send the link or set up a collaborative FaceTime call
- See all changes from teammates in **Latest Activity**
- Make changes to **Activity Settings** (see cursors, highlights) or **Manage Shared Presentation** (who has access)

![Sharing Options](/markdown/track_a/assets/sharing-options.png)

---

## Caveats and Warnings

- One student should create the "master document" first, before sharing
- Some features are not available when sharing on Keynote!
  - Changing theme
  - Changing slide size — this is important for the final high-fidelity prototype step
