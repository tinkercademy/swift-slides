## Create a Keynote Presentation

- For this section, please create a brand new Keynote presentation to try out the different techniques shown.
- In the presentation manager, tap the + button at the top of the screen.
- Select the "Choose a theme" option and choose the **Basic White** theme for your presentation
  - You can change the background colour for your app later!

---

<!-- .slide: class="layout-steps-media" -->
## Setting up your slides

- We'll need to change the size of the slides to mimic an iPad landscape app
- Tap on the document name at the top-left corner
  - Presentation Options
  - Presentation Setup
- Tap on Slide Size and select 3:4
- Note: This setup step is not available while collaborating!

![Setting up your slides](/markdown/track_a/assets/setting-up-unit6.png)

---

<!-- .slide: class="layout-steps-media" -->
## Adding new slides

- To add more screens to your prototype, you'll need to add slides
- Tap on the (add slide icon) at the bottom of the slide navigator and select the Blank layout
- To rearrange the slides, tap and hold a slide and drag it to the desired order

![Adding new slides](/markdown/track_a/assets/adding-new-slides.png)

---

## Icons and graphics

- Keynote has hundreds of free icons and graphics you can use as part of your prototype.
- Check it out by tapping on *Square and Circle Icon*
  - This is where you can add Text, under **Basic**
- You can also use emoji, for quick graphics 🆒🎉📱🐱🍪🤯📉😭

---

## Styling

- Select a piece of text by tapping on it
- Tap on the ![Paintbrush Icon](/markdown/track_a/assets/paintbrush-icon.png) icon
- Switch to the **Text** section
  - Adjust font and weight
- Try this button with shapes, too

---

## Adding images

- To add images, tap on (images icon)
- Tap on **Photo or Video**
- Select the image you would like to add
- Resize it with the corner drag handles once it's in

---

## Editing images

- To crop an image, double-tap the image and use the controls to frame the parts which you want to be visible
- To make part of an image transparent, tap on (paintbrush icon), then Image. Tap on **Remove Background**. Drag your finger over the colour you want to remove.

---

## Arranging Objects

- You might want to put some text on top of objects, or objects on top of other objects.
- Tap on an object, tap the (paintbrush icon) icon, and choose Arrange.
- The slider will let you choose how "in front" your object is.
- Here, you can also make other changes, like manual size and positioning changes, as well as rotation, flipping, and alignment options.

---

## Hyperlinks

- In Keynote, you can tap on an object and go to a specific slide, or even a website
- Hyperlinks are very important in prototypes, because they let users move between screens, rather than going from one slide to the next!

---

## Creating Hyperlinks

- To add a hyperlink, tap on the item and select **Link** from the pop-up menu (you might need to go to the next page with >).
- In the menu, choose **Link to Slide...**

---

## Linking Similar Screens

- If you link between two screens that look very similar, e.g. both share a common title bar, start by *duplicating* the page.
- When you switch between screens, Keynote will only animate the parts that are different across screens

## Magic Move

- Magic Move is an automatic transition which allows objects on one slide to move to new positions on the next slide
- To add a Magic Move transition, tap on the slide, then tap Transition
- Tap on Add Transition and select Magic Move

![Magic Move](/markdown/track_a/assets/keynote_transitions_magic_moves.png)
