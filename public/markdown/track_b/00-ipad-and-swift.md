## Taking a Screenshot

- Every lesson, we'll ask you to export a screenshot, so it's easier for us to take a look without having to connect to (and mess up) your iCloud version.
- On an iPad with a home button: Press the home and power buttons at the same time to take a screenshot.
- On an iPad without a home button: Quickly press and release the top button and either volume button at the same time.
- With Apple Pencil, swipe up diagonally from either corner at the bottom of the screen.

![Taking screenshots on iPads](/markdown/track_b/assets/ipad-screenshot.png)
![Taking screenshots on iPads without a Home button](/markdown/track_b/assets/ipad-screenshot-without-home-button.png)

## Recording your Screen

- First, ensure that the Screen Recording setting in Control Center is enabled
  - Go to Settings → Control Center, then tap the Add Button <img src="/markdown/track_b/assets/plus_button.svg" alt="Plus Button" height="24" style="vertical-align: middle;" /> next to Screen Recording
- Open the Control Center by swiping down from the top-right of the screen
- Tap the Record Button, wait for the three-second countdown. Then, tap it again to stop the recording

![Screen Recording](/markdown/track_b/assets/ipad-screenrecording-ss.png)
